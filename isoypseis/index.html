PRO

1. Drawing

Accepts DXF (points, lines, blocks) or TXT/CSV with "x y z". Load as many files as you like — all their layers are collected together.
Appearance
Colours are kept on this computer. They do not change the exported DXF.

2. Layer roles

Say what each layer is. Nothing is guessed from the names. Whatever stays "background" is simply drawn.
👁LayerRolePtsLinesZ

3. Calculation

A DXF LWPOLYLINE carries a single elevation for all of its vertices — it is flat. A line with real elevations has to be a 3D polyline or a series of LINE entities. If you drew it snapped onto the points, pick the second option.

Edits 0

Your file is never changed. The edits are kept separately and can all be undone.
The result is out of date — large drawing, the calculation does not run on its own.

4. Export

Drag to pan, wheel to zoom · click a point or a contour for its elevation.
